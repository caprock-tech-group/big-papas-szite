    Service controls

    Pace calls and triggers

    Green · 0–6 sold / 15 minUp to 24 per hour

    Keep 20–45 ready plus one batch cooking. Delay 10–15 minutes only if ready exceeds 45 and demand is falling.

    Yellow · 7–8 sold / 15 min28–32 per hour

    Bake nonstop. Protect the oven rhythm and stage the next 30.

    Red · 9+ sold / 15 min36+ per hour

    Above oven capacity. Quote waits, count queued orders, and do not run off-cycle topping reheats.

    Replenishment triggers

    • Potatoes: ready ≤30 or Yellow/Red pace → stage the next 30.
    • Hot toppings: ⅓ pan → replenish from hot backup only.
    • Meat, mac, gravy: ½ pan → replenish from hot backup.
    • Cold garnish / packaging: 25% remaining → restock.
    • Sell-through: ready + in oven ≤ paid/queued +10 → quote ETA or cap orders.
